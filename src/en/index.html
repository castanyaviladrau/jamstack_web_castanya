ABOUT THE PROJECT

THE ONLY 100 % CATALAN
ARTISAN CHESTNUT, 365 DAYS A YEAR

We are the only 100 % Catalan, artisan chestnut available all year round. From the heart of the Montseny Natural Park, Biosphere Reserve, we bring the forest to your plate while you help us care for it: every product supports sustainable forest management and the local economy of this Biosphere Reserve.
