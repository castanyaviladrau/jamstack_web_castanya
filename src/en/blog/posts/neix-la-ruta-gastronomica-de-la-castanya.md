At **La Castanya** we take a new step forward in our work to showcase this fruit as a product, a heritage, and a shared symbol. We are delighted to announce the launch of the **Chestnut Gastronomic Route**, a new initiative born to give visibility to this emblematic element through cuisine, experience and identity.

This route is not just a culinary itinerary; it is a living story that connects the forest with the table to explain who we are and how we understand our land.

## Joan Antonio: the first "Official Chestnut Chef"

The origin of this route begins with a particularly meaningful moment: the recognition of the chef of **Xalet La Coromina**, Joan Antonio, for his career, his gastronomic sensitivity and his love for the product.

We have awarded him the distinction of **Official Chestnut Chef**, a title that is far more than a tribute: it is a statement of intent. It marks the beginning of a path that seeks to give the chestnut the place it deserves — not only as a seasonal ingredient, but as a product with soul and with limitless gastronomic potential.

## Xalet La Coromina: a shared vision of the territory

At the start of this Route, we want to highlight the fundamental role of **Xalet La Coromina** (located in Viladrau). This venue perfectly embodies the vision we champion:

* **Commitment to our roots:** an honest, respectful cuisine that understands the restaurant as an extension of the landscape.
* **Sensitivity and craft:** Joan Antonio has known how to integrate the chestnut from a place of genuine affection, turning it into a language of its own within his culinary offering.
* **Vision for the future:** understanding that gastronomy is the most powerful tool to protect living heritage and our forests.

His involvement is not a one-off gesture, but a philosophy of life that makes him the ideal figure to symbolically open this new chapter.

---

## A project with soul and a path ahead

The **Chestnut Gastronomic Route** is born to bring people together, spark conversation and offer new ways to discover and savor a fruit that is part of our essence. This is only the beginning of a project that will keep growing, adding new venues and new perspectives, always guided by one clear idea: turning the chestnut into a living experience.

When we recognize someone who deeply loves a product, we not only celebrate their work; we also light the flame of a project capable of bringing even more life to everything around us.

**We invite you to rediscover the chestnut through your senses and to become part of this story that has only just begun.**
