At **Castanya de Viladrau** we have begun the recovery and maintenance work on the **nine-branch chestnut tree**, a singular tree that forms part of the natural, historical and emotional heritage of our territory.

This is not a one-off action or a quick intervention. It is, above all, the beginning of a **process of care, respect and commitment**, which we approach with the awareness that preserving a tree like this requires time, sensitivity and continuity. Restoring its surroundings, monitoring its condition and honoring its presence is work that must be understood with a long-term outlook.

The nine-branch chestnut tree is not just a tree. It is also a symbol. A presence that connects with the memory of Viladrau, with the culture of the chestnut tree, and with a way of understanding the landscape that goes far beyond its visual value. Caring for it is, in part, caring for a shared history.

This opportunity to contribute to its recovery moves us and makes us feel responsible. At **Castanya de Viladrau**, we take on this task as a natural continuation of the work we do every day: showcasing the value of the chestnut, the chestnut tree, and everything it represents within the territory. Not only as a product, but as a legacy, an identity and a root.

The work that is now beginning aims to help **organize, maintain, accompany and preserve** this space with the respect it deserves. We know it will be a long road, but we deeply believe in the importance of walking it. Because there are elements of the landscape that should not only be admired: they must also be cared for.

With this step, we begin a task that we hope will serve not only to preserve an emblematic tree, but also to strengthen the bond between heritage, nature and community. The nine-branch chestnut tree is part of the soul of this territory, and that is why we understand that caring for it is also a way of looking to the future without losing our roots.
