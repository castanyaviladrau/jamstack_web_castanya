The “Sureda” beer is more than just a beer. Drawing on the ingredients of the Mediterranean landscape of a cork oak forest, we set out not to make just another beer, but to find the umami of our beer.

We have crafted an Extra Strong Catalan Ale: a beer that embodies a territory, a culture, a gastronomy and a way of working and doing things.

It brings together the diversity of textures, flavours and nuances, the reason and the passion of our land, to ultimately become “the beer”.

After a long process of growing and transforming its ingredients, everything culminates in the brewing process of our beers, respecting the stages of pressing, resting, fermentation, storage and maturation. All of this contributes to its round, complex and pure flavour, with the presence of yeasts, preserving the flavours, aromas and qualities of a natural beer.
