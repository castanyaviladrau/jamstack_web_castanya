THE SOUL OF THE MONTSENY IN YOUR HOME

We create bespoke projects with chestnut wood from the Montseny, the ideal solution for indoors and outdoors. Thanks to its tannins, our debarked wood is naturally resistant and 100 % toxin-free. A zero-mile choice that cares for your health, restores the forest and helps prevent fires.

WHY DON'T WE NEED CHEMICALS?

Chestnut wood is naturally resistant thanks to its tannins, which protect it from fungi and insects without the need for any chemical treatment. This self-protection makes it a 100 % toxin-free option, guaranteeing a healthy, warm indoor environment full of authenticity. As a zero-mile product, it reduces environmental impact and encourages the active forest management that is vital for fire prevention in the Montseny. Choosing this noble wood means connecting with the artisan tradition of Viladrau and giving value to a living heritage that cares for our land.
