ingredientsLabel: INGREDIENTS
ingredients:
  - 300 g peeled, cooked chestnuts
  - 200 g sugar
  - 1 egg yolk
  - 150 g pine nuts
  - Lemon zest
downloadPdfLabel: DOWNLOAD RECIPE
pdfEnabled: true
steps:
  - title: STEP 1
    text: Cook the chestnuts until very tender. Once cooked, drain them and blend until you get a smooth, even paste.
  - title: STEP 2
    text: Add the sugar, egg yolk and lemon zest to the chestnut paste. Mix well until all the ingredients are fully combined.
  - title: STEP 3
    text: Shape small balls with the dough, keeping them an even size.
  - title: STEP 4
    text: Coat the panellets with the pine nuts, pressing lightly so they stick well to the dough.
  - title: STEP 5
    text: Preheat the oven to 180 °C and place the panellets on a tray lined with baking paper.
  - title: STEP 6
    text: Bake the panellets for 10-15 minutes, until lightly golden. Let them cool before serving.

secretQuote: To help the pine nuts stick better, brush the panellets lightly with egg yolk before coating them. If you let the dough rest in the fridge, it will be easier to work with and the panellets will hold together better.

A different, delicious take on traditional panellets, where chestnut lends a soft texture and a naturally sweet flavour with a very special autumn touch. Perfect for sharing at the Castanyada.
