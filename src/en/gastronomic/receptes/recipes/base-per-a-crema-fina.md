steps:
  - title: STEP 1
    text: Sauté the spring onion over low heat until translucent and sweet. This step builds an aromatic base that accompanies the chestnut without overpowering it.
  - title: STEP 2
    text: Add the boiled chestnut and moisten with hot vegetable stock. Cook for a few minutes until all the flavours are well combined.
  - title: STEP 3
    text: Blend until smooth and adjust the thickness with more stock if needed. Serve it as a base for other dishes or as a light cream.
