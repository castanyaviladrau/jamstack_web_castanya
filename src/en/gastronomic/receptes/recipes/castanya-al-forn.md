steps:
  - title: STEP 1
    text: Score the chestnuts with a clean cut and spread them out on a tray. This prevents them from bursting and makes them easier to peel afterwards.
  - title: STEP 2
    text: Roast them at a high temperature until the skin splits open and they give off a toasted aroma. You can drizzle them lightly with oil to help them brown.
  - title: STEP 3
    text: Let them rest covered with a cloth for a few minutes before serving. This keeps them warm and makes the skin come away more easily.
