ingredients:
  - 250 g minced pork + 250 g minced beef
  - 300 g cooked, peeled chestnuts
  - 300 g mixed wild mushrooms
  - 3-4 tablespoons onion confit
  - 1 carrot and 1 ripe tomato
  - 1 glass of fortified wine and meat stock
  - Breadcrumbs, milk, egg, parsley, flour
  - Extra virgin olive oil, salt and black pepper

steps:
  - title: STEP 1
    text: Soak the bread crumb in the milk until nicely softened. In a large bowl, mix the minced pork and beef with the chopped garlic, parsley, egg, drained bread crumb, salt and black pepper.
  - title: STEP 2
    text: Work the mixture until all the ingredients are well combined. Shape the meatballs, dust them lightly with flour and fry them in a pan with extra virgin olive oil until golden. Set them aside on paper towel.
  - title: STEP 3
    text: In a pot with olive oil, sauté the finely diced carrot, the grated tomato and the chopped garlic cloves.
  - title: STEP 4
    text: Add the onion confit and the glass of fortified wine. Let it reduce for a few minutes over low heat so the alcohol evaporates and the sauce gains intensity.
  - title: STEP 5
    text: For a thicker sauce, add a teaspoon of flour and stir well. Moisten with a little meat stock and let it simmer gently for a few minutes.
  - title: STEP 6
    text: Add the meatballs, the cooked, peeled chestnuts and the mixed wild mushrooms. Let everything cook together for 10-12 minutes over low heat, so the meatballs soak up the sauce nicely.
  - title: STEP 7
    text: Turn off the heat and let the dish rest for a few minutes before serving. If made a day ahead, it will taste even better.

A tasty, traditional and deeply comforting autumn recipe, ideal for All Saints' lunch or for any special seasonal meal. Tender meatballs are combined with chestnuts and wild mushrooms in a sauce full of depth, with the aromatic touch of fortified wine and the flavour of slow-cooked food.
