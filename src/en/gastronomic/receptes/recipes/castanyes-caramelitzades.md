ingredients:
  - 150 g peeled chestnuts
  - 150 g honey
  - 100 g sugar
  - 150 ml water
  - 1/2 cinnamon stick
  - Extra virgin olive oil
downloadPdfLabel: DOWNLOAD RECIPE
pdfEnabled: true
steps:
  - title: STEP 1
    text: Score the chestnuts and leave them to soak for 10 minutes.
  - title: STEP 2
    text: Without changing containers, cover them with water and microwave on full power for 10 minutes.
  - title: STEP 3
    text: Once they've cooled slightly, peel them. Then place them in a pot with the honey, sugar and cinnamon, and let them simmer gently for 2 minutes.
  - title: STEP 4
    text: Remove from the heat and let cool. Put the pot back on the heat for another 2 minutes. Repeat this process twice so the chestnuts absorb the caramel well.
  - title: STEP 5
    text: Once cooking is finished, let the chestnuts cool. Finally, lightly fry them in a pan with extra virgin olive oil until nicely golden.

Caramelised chestnuts are a traditional, surprisingly simple recipe that turns the chestnut into a small, irresistible sweet bite, perfect for sharing at the Castanyada or enjoying as an autumn treat.
