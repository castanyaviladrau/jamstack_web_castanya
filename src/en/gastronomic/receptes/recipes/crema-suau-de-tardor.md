steps:
  - title: STEP 1
    text: Warm a little milk and dissolve the chestnut flour into it gradually to avoid lumps. Getting an even base is key to the final texture.
  - title: STEP 2
    text: Add the mild stock and let it cook over very low heat, stirring constantly. The cream will gradually thicken without losing its lightness.
  - title: STEP 3
    text: Finish with a touch of nutmeg and adjust the texture before serving. You can top it with some crumbled chestnuts.
