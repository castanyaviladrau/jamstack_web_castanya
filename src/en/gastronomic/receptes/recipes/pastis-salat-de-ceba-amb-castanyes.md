ingredientsLabel: INGREDIENTS
ingredients:
  - 200 g peeled chestnuts
  - 2 red onions
  - 2 eggs and 200 ml liquid cream
  - Aromatic herbs (chives and parsley)
  - 1 sheet of puff pastry
  - 4 tablespoons breadcrumbs
  - Extra virgin olive oil
  - Salt and black pepper
downloadPdfLabel: DOWNLOAD RECIPE
pdfEnabled: true
steps:
  - title: STEP 1
    text: Cut the chestnuts into thin slices. If using frozen chestnuts, let them thaw completely. Peel the red onions and cut them into thin rings.
  - title: STEP 2
    text: Heat the olive oil in a large pan and sauté the chestnuts for about 5 minutes. Add the red onion and cook briefly, until it starts to soften. Season to taste and let cool.
  - title: STEP 3
    text: In a bowl, beat the eggs with the liquid cream. Add the finely chopped aromatic herbs and season with salt and black pepper. Preheat the oven to 220 °C.
  - title: STEP 4
    text: Place the sheet of puff pastry, with its baking paper, on a tray. Prick the whole surface with a fork so it doesn't puff up too much during baking.
  - title: STEP 5
    text: Scatter the breadcrumbs over the puff pastry base. On top, spread the chestnut and red onion mixture, then pour the egg, cream and herb mixture over it.
  - title: STEP 6
    text: Bake the tart on the lower rack of the oven for about 20-22 minutes, until the pastry is golden and the filling is set. Let it rest for a few minutes before slicing.

A simple, aromatic and highly versatile savoury tart, ideal as a starter, a light dinner or a dish to share. The combination of red onion and chestnuts brings a delicate, sweet contrast, while the aromatic herbs add freshness and balance.
