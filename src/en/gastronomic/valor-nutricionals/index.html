PROPERTIES AND NUTRITIONAL VALUES: THE SUPERFOOD OF THE MONTSENY

More than an autumn pleasure, Castanya de Viladrau is a source of healthy energy, rich in fibre and gluten-free.

QUALITY ENERGY
(COMPLEX CARBOHYDRATES)

Unlike other nuts, the chestnut is rich in slow-release carbohydrates. This makes it ideal for athletes and children, as it provides steady energy without sugar spikes.

NATURALLY GLUTEN-FREE

Our chestnut and its flour are the perfect alternative for people with coeliac disease or gluten sensitivity. It's an excellent base for breads, cakes and sauces.

LOW IN FAT

Despite being a forest fruit, the chestnut contains very little fat, only 2–3 %, making it much lighter than walnuts or almonds.

RICH IN FIBRE AND POTASSIUM

It aids digestion and contributes to cardiovascular health. It's also a natural source of vitamin C and antioxidants, especially when eaten fresh or lightly cooked.
