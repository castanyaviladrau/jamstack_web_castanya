This page summarises the shipping and pickup conditions applicable to orders placed in the Castanya de Viladrau online shop.

## Shipping area

Currently, Castanya de Viladrau ships within Spanish territory. The delivery details entered during the checkout process must be correct and complete to ensure the order arrives without issues.

## Shipping cost

Shipments require a minimum order of **€50.00**. Orders with in-store pickup can be processed below this amount.

If the order amount does not reach **€50.00** and you prefer home delivery, a shipping cost of **€11.40** will be added. This amount is calculated automatically during the checkout process and is shown before confirming the order.

## In-store pickup

If you prefer to collect your order at one of our shops, you can enable this option during checkout to avoid the shipping cost. Billing does not change in this case.

## Delivery times

Once confirmed, an order normally arrives within **24 to 48 hours**. This timeframe may vary during periods of high demand, public holidays or for reasons beyond Castanya de Viladrau's control.

## Issues during transport

If a product arrives damaged or incomplete as a result of transport, please follow the procedure described on the **Returns** page, notifying us at **info@castanyadeviladrau.cat** within a maximum of **7 business days** of receipt.

## Liability

Castanya de Viladrau is not responsible for delays or delivery failures resulting from incorrect details, the recipient's absence or causes beyond its control.
