The guided route is an invitation to look at the forest with different eyes. We walk at an unhurried pace through an environment where every tree tells part of the local history and the recovery work we carry out in Montseny.

Along the way we share anecdotes, curiosities and insights to help you understand why the centenary chestnut trees are an essential part of Viladrau's cultural landscape.

### What will we do during the visit?

- We'll walk among monumental trees and identify the most characteristic features of the chestnut forest.
- We'll talk about biodiversity, traditional use of resources and active forest management.
- We'll close the experience with a tasting designed to connect landscape with flavor.

This is an ideal proposal for couples, small groups and people looking for an authentic, personal experience full of context.
