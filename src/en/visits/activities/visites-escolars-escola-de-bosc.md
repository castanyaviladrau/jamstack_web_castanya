School visits aim to bring the forest closer to children and young people through an active, sensitive experience rooted in the local area. It's not just about explaining content, but about experiencing it firsthand.

We work with groups looking for an outing with educational purpose, a connection with nature, and a safe space to explore the relationship between landscape, biodiversity and traditional trades.

### Goals of the program

- Spark curiosity about the natural environment and the chestnut cycle.
- Understand the role of the forest and its management in the local area.
- Foster observation, teamwork and respect for the environment.
