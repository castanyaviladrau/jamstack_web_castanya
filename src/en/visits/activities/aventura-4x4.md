Come and live an immersive experience in the heart of Montseny to get to know up close the culture of a fruit that is part of our identity: the Viladrau chestnut. This activity is an invitation to disconnect from the frantic pace of everyday life and connect with the silence and wisdom of the forests.

### What will we do during the visit?

* **Guided walk through the forest:** We explore our centenary chestnut trees to understand the value of active forest management that preserves the landscape and biodiversity of the area.
* **Living heritage:** We discover the natural cycle of the chestnut tree, from flowering to timber, and how artisanal work sustains the memory of this unique ecosystem within the Biosphere Reserve.
* **Tasting of crafted products:** We end the experience with an exclusive tasting of our chestnut-based products, made with locally sourced ingredients and gastronomic sensibility.

An ideal outing for lovers of nature and real gastronomy who want to discover the origin of everything that reaches their plate and the importance of caring for our environment.
