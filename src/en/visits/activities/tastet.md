The tasting is a direct and flavorful way to enter the world of the Viladrau chestnut. In a short time, we condense the essence of the project into a carefully crafted, welcoming tasting.

The session is designed for curious people, small groups and visitors looking for a first gastronomic approach to our work.

### You will find

- Sweet and savory products made with chestnut.
- Simple explanations about origin, processing and possible uses.
- An agile format, ideal for complementing other activities or short visits.
