The estate is where the story of the forest takes shape. Here we explain where we come from, how we work, and the outlook behind every decision linked to the Viladrau chestnut.

This is an experience designed for those who want to understand the project at a relaxed pace, put a face to the processes, and discover how the land, craftsmanship and gastronomy can go hand in hand.

### During the visit you will discover

* The history and evolution of the project.
* The different product formats and their artisanal logic.
* The connection between forest recovery, food transformation and local culture.
