WELCOME TO THE HEART OF A
LAND THAT BREATHES.

The Montseny Natural Park is not just a protected space; it is a living, ever-changing and eternal setting. Declared a Biosphere Reserve by UNESCO, this territory represents the perfect balance between people and nature. Here, our passion is not only to work the land, but to listen to it and become part of its pulse.

A BOND WITH THE LAND:
MORE THAN A LANDSCAPE

Understanding the Montseny means understanding the deep bond that ties us to the territory. These mountains are a haven of biodiversity unique in southern Europe.

Our mission is born from an absolute admiration for this environment. We don't see ourselves as owners of the forest, but as its temporary guardians. Every visit, every step we take along its paths, is a tribute to the natural richness of Viladrau and its springs, its silences and its geological history. Coming here means disconnecting from the noise to reconnect with what matters most.

THE GIANTS OF THE FOREST:
CENTURIES-OLD CHESTNUT TREES

If the Montseny had memory, it would live within the bark of its trees. Our territory is home to numerous centuries-old chestnut trees, true natural monuments that have seen generations come and go.

These trees are not mere vegetation; they are living heritage of humanity. Some of them have been rooted on these slopes for centuries, withstanding storms and droughts. Walking among them is like strolling through an open-air museum where nature is the architect. Their majesty reminds us how small we are and that time, here, moves to a different rhythm.

RECOVERY: RESCUING THE
HISTORY OF THE MONTSENY

Love for the land is proven through action. That's why we have devoted our efforts to rescuing and restoring chestnut trees that were in danger of disappearing, forgotten beneath the closed forest canopy.

This recovery work goes beyond forest management; it is an act of cultural and environmental responsibility. By clearing the forest and caring for these old specimens:

We preserve the genetics of unique trees.

We reduce the risk of fire, protecting the Biosphere Reserve.

We bring light back to the undergrowth, allowing life to be reborn.

WHY DISCOVER THE MONTSENY
THROUGH OUR TOURS?

We don't just invite you to see trees, but to live an immersive experience. Our guided tours are designed for those seeking authenticity.

We want you to feel the dampness of the earth, the scent of dry leaves and the grandeur of these restored chestnut trees. It's an opportunity to learn why this corner of the world is so special and why its conservation is vital for the future.
