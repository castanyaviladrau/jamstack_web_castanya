This website uses cookies and similar technologies to ensure the site's basic functioning, remember consent preferences and manage third-party content when users accept it.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on a user's device when they visit a website. Among other functions, they are used to remember preferences, facilitate navigation and obtain aggregated statistical information.

## Types of cookies that may be used

- technical or necessary cookies: essential for the basic functioning of the website
- preference cookies: remember certain browsing options
- third-party cookies: may come from external services integrated into the website

## Consent management

When accessing the website, a banner is shown allowing users to accept or reject non-essential cookies. This choice is saved in the browser via `localStorage` to avoid showing the notice again on every visit.

Users can change this choice at any time via the `Change cookies` link available in the footer.

Currently, Google Maps are only loaded once the user has given consent. If consent is rejected or withdrawn, the maps are blocked again. The website's main typeface is hosted locally and does not depend on Google Fonts.

## How to disable or delete cookies

Users can configure their browser to block or delete cookies at any time. This configuration may affect some of the website's functionality.

## More information

If new third-party services are added or there are relevant changes to the cookies used, this policy will be updated to reflect them.
